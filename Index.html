Inside the Bottle

Three ingredients that make the difference

Every ingredient was chosen for its direct effect on skin health — no filler, nothing unnecessary.

Jojoba (Simondia chinensis) Seed Oil

  • Deep Hydration: Locks moisture into the skin without clogging pores, since it closely resembles skin's natural sebum.
  • Fights Acne: Reduces breakouts and balances oil production thanks to its antibacterial and anti-inflammatory properties, without causing blackheads.
  • Soothes Skin: Reduces redness and irritation caused by dry skin or conditions like eczema.
  • Fights Wrinkles: Rich in vitamin E and antioxidants that fight fine lines and support skin elasticity.

Macadamia (Macadamia Ternifolia) Seed Oil

  • Fights Aging: Contains palmitoleic acid and vitamin E, which stimulate skin cell renewal and reduce the appearance of wrinkles and fine lines.
  • Protects Skin: Forms a light barrier that shields skin from sun damage and environmental stress, helping even out skin tone.
  • Gentle on Sensitive Skin: Its lightweight, non-comedogenic texture won't clog pores, making it suitable even for irritated or sensitive skin.
  • Repairs Damage: Helps reduce the appearance of minor scars and boosts skin elasticity and youthfulness.

Sodium Hyaluronate

  • Deep Hydration: Holds up to 1000 times its weight in water, giving skin intense, long-lasting moisture.
  • Reduces Wrinkles: Helps fill in fine lines and gaps, minimizing the visible signs of aging.
  • Boosts Elasticity: Strengthens the skin's natural barrier and improves its elasticity and resistance to dryness.
